Computer System

Introduction of Computer

A computer is an electronic programmable device that accepts data as input, processes it according to a set of instructions, stores the data and produces meaningful information as output. It can perform complex calculations and logical operations in a very short period of time.

Computers are widely used in our daily lives. They are used in education, business, banking, communication, healthcare, entertainment, transportation, science and many other fields.

Applications of Computer

  1. Education: Computers are used for online classes, presentations, digital learning and examinations.
  2. Banking: Computers are used for maintaining accounts, online banking and financial transactions.
  3. Healthcare: Computers are used to maintain patient records, conduct research and assist in medical diagnosis.
  4. Business: Computers are used for accounting, communication, inventory management and data processing.
  5. Communication: Computers help people communicate through email, video calls and social media.
  6. Entertainment: Computers are used for playing games, watching movies, listening to music and creating digital content.
  7. Science and Technology: Computers are used for scientific research, calculations and simulations.
  8. Transportation: Computers are used for ticket booking, navigation and traffic management.

Limitations of Computer

  • A computer cannot think or make decisions like a human.
  • It works according to the instructions given to it.
  • Incorrect input may produce incorrect results.
  • A computer requires electricity to operate.
  • A computer does not have emotions or common sense.
