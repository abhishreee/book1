Multimedia

7.1 Introduction to Multimedia

Multimedia is the combination of different forms of media such as text, graphics, audio, video and animation to present information in an attractive and interactive way. It is widely used in education, entertainment, business, advertising, communication and websites.

7.2 Components of Multimedia

The major components of multimedia are text, graphics, audio, video and animation. Text is used to provide written information, graphics represent information through images, audio provides sound, video presents moving visual information, and animation creates the appearance of movement. These components work together to make information more effective and interesting.

7.3 Text

Text is one of the basic components of multimedia and is used to communicate information through written words. A text editor is a software used to create and modify text. Font type refers to the design of characters, while font style describes their appearance, such as bold or italic. Font size determines the size of characters. Formatting includes changing the alignment, spacing and appearance of text. Color can be used to make text attractive and easy to notice. Typography is the arrangement and design of text to make it clear, readable and visually appealing.

7.4 Graphics

Graphics are visual representations used to communicate information or make multimedia content attractive. Bitmap graphics are made up of small dots called pixels and are commonly used for photographs. Vector graphics are created using mathematical shapes and can be resized without losing quality. 2D graphics have two dimensions, height and width, whereas 3D graphics have height, width and depth and provide a more realistic appearance.

7.5 Audio

Digital audio is the representation of sound in digital form so that it can be stored and processed by computers. MIDI (Musical Instrument Digital Interface) stores musical instructions rather than actual recorded sound. WAV is a high-quality audio format, while MP3 is a compressed format that requires less storage space. AU is an audio file format commonly associated with UNIX systems. Real Audio is designed mainly for streaming audio over the Internet.

7.6 Video

Digital video is the representation of moving images in digital form. A video is made up of a series of individual pictures called frames, which are displayed rapidly to create the appearance of motion. MPEG is a common standard for compressing digital video. AVI is a video file format developed by Microsoft, while MOV was developed by Apple. MPG is another commonly used extension for MPEG video files.

7.7 Animation

Animation is the technique of creating the illusion of movement by displaying a series of images or objects in sequence. It is widely used in education, games, advertisements, movies and websites. Animation can be created using different tools and software. Animated GIF is a simple image format that supports a sequence of images to produce basic animation. Flash was a multimedia technology used to create animations and interactive content for the web.
